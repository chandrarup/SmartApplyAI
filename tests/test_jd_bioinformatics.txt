Role: Senior Computational Biologist / Spatial Omics Scientist
Company: NanoString / 10x Genomics
Location: Boston, MA (or Remote)

About the Job:
We are looking for a Computational Biologist / Spatial Omics Scientist to develop statistical models and analysis pipelines for high-plex single-cell spatial transcriptomics data. You will analyze CosMx and Xenium imaging datasets, perform cell segmentation, and conduct differential gene expression profiling.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build analysis pipelines in Python and R using tools such as Scanpy, Seurat, and spatial statistical modeling.
- Perform cell typing and spatial clustering on high-dimensional single-cell datasets (100k+ cells across hundreds of genes).
- Implement deep learning computer vision models (CellPose, StarDist) on multi-gigabyte fluorescence microscopy TIFF images.
- Develop interactive visual analytics platforms (FastAPI, HTML5 Canvas) for biomedical collaborators.
- Collaborate with cross-functional wet-lab and pathology teams to translate spatial findings into clinical insights.
- M.S. or Ph.D. in Computational Biology, Biomedical Engineering, Bioinformatics, or Computer Science.
