## Empirical Foundations of Recruitment Screening and Qualification Proof

### Deconstructing Applicant Tracking Systems and Recruiter Checklists

Large-scale application analyses published in [Tsenta's Interview Conversion Study](https://dashboard.tsenta.com/dashboard/research/interviews) and [Tsenta's Applicant Tracking System Analysis](https://dashboard.tsenta.com/dashboard/research/ats-myths) refute the widespread belief that Applicant Tracking Systems (such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, and Workable) automatically reject 75% of candidates using secret keyword-density scores. In practice, ATS platforms function as compliance and workflow repositories. Automated rejections occur almost exclusively through explicit **knockout questions** configured by the employer on the intake form—specifically degree completion windows, work authorization or visa sponsorship requirements, geographic relocation rules, and mandatory security clearances.

Once an application passes the intake form, a human recruiter—who is frequently a non-technical generalist operating from a hiring manager's intake rubric—scans the rendered PDF for 6 to 15 seconds. In a competitive early-career and mid-level market where a well-positioned candidate converts roughly **1 interview per 100 to 200 applications** ([Tsenta Interview Conversion Study](https://dashboard.tsenta.com/dashboard/research/interviews)), applications fail during this human scan for two primary reasons:

1. **Archetype Dilution ("Serving the Hot Dog Before the Hamburger"):** When a candidate applies to diverse roles (such as Agentic AI Software Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Core Machine Learning Engineer, Bioinformatics Scientist, and Data Analyst) using a single unified resume, the top third of the page inevitably highlights domain work irrelevant to the target role. Placing lupus nephritis spatial transcriptomics at the top of a resume submitted for a backend Agentic AI role causes the screener to categorize the applicant as an academic biologist rather than a production software engineer.
2. **Unverified Keyword Claims ("A Keyword is Not a Qualification"):** Listing `LangGraph`, `Model Context Protocol (MCP)`, `FastAPI`, `Docker`, `Kubernetes`, `PySpark`, or `Java` in a bottom `Skills` block while omitting them from the work experience bullets results in a zero score on the recruiter's checklist.

### The Four-Dimensional Qualification Proof Model

To convert a raw keyword from a job description into a verified qualification that passes both non-technical recruiter checklists and senior engineering screens, every resume bullet generated by the synthesis engine must satisfy four structural dimensions ([Tsenta Interview Conversion Study](https://dashboard.tsenta.com/dashboard/research/interviews)):

$$\text{Qualification Proof} = \underbrace{\text{WHAT}}_{\substack{\text{Exact JD Term +}\\\text{Base Language}}} \oplus \underbrace{\text{HOW}}_{\substack{\text{System, Architecture,}\\\text{or Pipeline Built}}} \oplus \underbrace{\text{WHY}}_{\substack{\text{Plain-English Business}\\\text{or Scientific Outcome}}} \oplus \underbrace{\text{WHERE}}_{\substack{\text{Specific Role or}\\\text{Project Entry}}}$$

| Dimension | Structural Requirement | Defective Implementation (Fails Screen) | Verified Implementation (Passes Screen) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WHAT** | Mirrors the exact terminology from the JD and explicitly pairs every framework with its underlying programming language or query language. | *"Used LangGraph and FastAPI for backend services."* (Omits `Python` and `REST APIs`). | *"Built production **REST APIs** and **agent-to-agent (A2A)** workflows in **Python** using **FastAPI** and **LangGraph**..."* |
| **HOW** | Describes the concrete engineering mechanism, data pipeline, model architecture, or integration layer constructed. | *"Led Change Impact Analysis for enterprise clients."* (Vague leadership claim). | *"...combining LLM dependency graphs and hybrid **RAG** over **PostgreSQL (pgvector)**, **Pinecone**, and **Neo4j**..."* |
| **WHY** | Explains in plain English what the system enabled, prevented, or saved so a non-technical screener grasps the value immediately. | *"...improving system performance by 89%."* (Contextless percentage). | *"...so enterprise teams could automate impact reviews across **30,000+ files** and **15+ languages**, cutting manual review from weeks to hours at **89% coverage**."* |
| **WHERE** | Anchors the proof inside a dated chronological work experience or named project entry rather than a summary or skills list. | Listing `MCP`, `Docker`, and `GKE` only in the bottom `Skills` block. | Embedding `MCP` inside the **Accenture** and **UH Bauer** bullets and `Docker`/`GKE` inside the **Accenture** and **Hush** bullets. |

### The Eight Structural Rules Enforced by the Synthesis Engine

The automated resume generator enforces eight non-negotiable rules derived from [Tsenta's Interview Conversion Study](https://dashboard.tsenta.com/dashboard/research/interviews):

1. **Bijective Skill-to-Bullet Grounding:** Every high-priority technology listed in the bottom `Skills` section must appear in at least one `Experience` or `Selected Projects` bullet. Standalone skill tokens are treated as unverified claims until proved in a bullet.
2. **Archetype-Driven Section Ordering:** The top 40% of the page must immediately deliver the primary qualification archetype ("the hamburger") before introducing secondary cross-disciplinary strengths ("the hot dog").
3. **First-Bullet Primacy:** The first bullet of the first listed role must prove the single highest-weighted requirement of the target job description.
4. **Deterministic Base-Language Expansion:** Human recruiters do not infer programming languages from frameworks. Whenever a framework is injected, the engine automatically injects its base language according to a strict dependency table (`FastAPI`, `Flask`, `PyTorch`, `Scikit-learn`, `LangChain`, `LangGraph`, `Scanpy` $\rightarrow$ `Python`; `React`, `Next.js`, `TanStack Start` $\rightarrow$ `TypeScript, JavaScript`; `PostgreSQL`, `pgvector`, `Supabase`, `SQLite` $\rightarrow$ `SQL`).
5. **Dual-Audience Readability:** Every dense technical noun (such as `InSituType negative-binomial likelihood`, `BERTopic`, `GRPO`, or `SHAP`) must be paired in the same sentence with a plain-English outcome clause (`"so wet-lab biologists could inspect tissue cohorts without writing code"` or `"to surface high-risk turnover segments for executive retention planning"`).
6. **Concrete Engineering Action Verbs:** Generic management verbs (*"Spearheaded"*, *"Synergized"*, *"Drove"*, *"Utilized"*, *"Leveraged"*) are banned. Every bullet opens with a specific engineering verb (*"Built"*, *"Architected"*, *"Engineered"*, *"Implemented"*, *"Fine-tuned"*, *"Modeled"*, *"Integrated"*).
7. **Contextualized Scale Metrics:** Quantitative figures are included only when paired with physical scale units (`30,000+ files`, `15+ languages`, `89% coverage`, `289,000-row employee panel`, `ROC-AUC 0.714`, `532,392 cells × 960 genes`, `11 patient cohorts`, `5GB microscopy TIFFs`, `100M+ parameters`, `96% classification accuracy`).
8. **Collaborative & Operational Ownership:** Early-career and senior-associate roles screen heavily for team integration. At least two bullets per resume must prove cross-functional collaboration, mentoring (`15+ junior engineers`), Agile/Scrum delivery, or stakeholder communication (`60+ MBA students`, `Humana RN, strategy VP, and physicians`).

## End-to-End Multi-Stage Software Architecture for Antigravity

To build a standalone software application in **Antigravity** that accepts a raw job description (or job URL) and outputs a compiled, single-page tailored resume in both `.tex` and `.pdf` formats, the system is structured as a **six-stage deterministic pipeline** with a closed-loop compilation controller:

| Pipeline Stage | Core Software Module | Input Schema | Output Schema | Failure Recovery / Guardrail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Ingestion** | `jd_ingestor.py` | Job URL or raw pasted JD text | `RawJobPosting` (clean text, company, title, location) | Falls back from static HTTP fetch to Headless Chromium DOM extraction for Single-Page Applications (Workday, Ashby, Greenhouse). |
| **Stage 2: Deconstruction** | `jd_analyzer.py` | `RawJobPosting` | `DeconstructedJD` (Pydantic model with ranked hard skills, base languages, archetype weights, knockout flags) | Flags hard eligibility blockers (such as US Citizenship Required or Graduation $\ge$ Fall 2027) before spending tokens on generation. |
| **Stage 3: Retrieval** | `knowledge_graph.py` | `DeconstructedJD` + `career_knowledge.json` | `SelectedEvidenceBundle` (ordered section list, role framing variants, top 3–4 projects) | Restricts the LLM strictly to verified entities, dates, and metrics stored in `career_knowledge.json`; forbids inventing unbuilt tools. |
| **Stage 4: Synthesis** | `bullet_synthesizer.py` | `SelectedEvidenceBundle` + `DeconstructedJD` | `TailoredResumeAST` (JSON abstract syntax tree of all sections, headers, bullets, and skills) | Runs a post-generation regex & token check ensuring every bullet is $\le 245$ characters (2 rendered lines) and contains no banned AI-slop verbs. |
| **Stage 5: Compilation** | `latex_compiler.py` | `TailoredResumeAST` | Verified `.tex` and 1-page `.pdf` + 150-DPI `.png` preview | Executes up to 4 closed-loop geometry/compression iterations until `Pages == 1`, `Overfull \hbox == 0`, and vertical fill is $\ge 90\%$. |
| **Stage 6: Audit** | `fit_auditor.py` | Verified `.tex` + `DeconstructedJD` | `FitAuditReport` (Strong matches, reframed matches, true gaps, ATS safety check) | Surfaces any true JD skill gaps honestly so the user knows which gaps could be asked about in an interview. |

### Mathematical Formulation of the Closed-Loop Single-Page Controller

Let $H_{\text{page}} = 792\text{ pt}$ ($11\text{ in}$) and $W_{\text{page}} = 612\text{ pt}$ ($8.5\text{ in}$) denote the dimensions of a US Letter page. With top/bottom margins $m_y \in [0.22\text{ in}, 0.28\text{ in}]$ and left/right margins $m_x \in [0.35\text{ in}, 0.38\text{ in}]$, the printable text height is $H_{\text{print}} = H_{\text{page}} - 2m_y$.

After rasterizing Page 1 to an RGB image $I(x, y)$ of height $H_{\text{px}}$ and width $W_{\text{px}}$, the controller scans upward from the bottom margin boundary $y_{\max}$ to locate the lowest non-white ink pixel $y_{\text{ink}}$ (where $\min(R, G, B) < 245$):

$$\Phi_{\text{fill}} = \frac{y_{\text{ink}} - y_{\min}}{y_{\max} - y_{\min}} \times 100\%$$

The document passes verification if and only if all four deterministic constraints hold simultaneously:

$$\begin{cases}
N_{\text{pages}} = 1 & \text{(Strict Single-Page Constraint)} \\
90.0\% \le \Phi_{\text{fill}} \le 98.5\% & \text{(Balanced Vertical Density Constraint)} \\
N_{\text{overfull\_hbox}} = 0 \text{ (or } \Delta w_{\max} \le 2.0\text{ pt)} & \text{(Zero Horizontal Margin Overhang)} \\
N_{\text{wrapped\_proj\_headers}} = 0 & \text{(Single-Line Project Header Constraint)}
\end{cases}$$

### Four-Step Automated Layout Repair Algorithm

Whenever a compiled draft violates any of the four constraints above, the closed-loop controller executes deterministic repairs before delivering the PDF:

1. **Case A — Horizontal Overhang or Wrapped Project Header ($\Delta w > 2.0\text{ pt}$):**
   - **Cause 1:** A `\projheader{Title | Tech Stack}{Year}` line exceeds the printable width ($540\text{ pt}$), pushing the italicized `Year` onto a second orphan line.
   - **Fix 1:** Automatically abbreviates verbose stack tokens in the project header (for example, `Kubernetes (GKE)` $\rightarrow$ `GKE`, `Hugging Face Transformers` $\rightarrow$ `Hugging Face`, `LoRA/PEFT` $\rightarrow$ `LoRA`) until the header + year fit on a single line ($\le 92$ characters).
   - **Cause 2:** An underlined hyperlink `\ulhref{URL}{\textbf{long multi-word phrase}}` occurs near the right margin (in LaTeX, `\underline{...}` creates an unbreakable horizontal box `\hbox` that cannot hyphenate or wrap across lines).
   - **Fix 2:** Restricts `\ulhref{URL}{...}` to the concise 2–3 word proper noun (e.g., `\textbf{100M+ parameter} \ulhref{...}{\textbf{GPT-2 model}}` instead of underlining the entire 7-word phrase).
2. **Case B — Minor Vertical Spillover ($N_{\text{pages}} = 2$ with $\le 3$ spillover lines on Page 2):**
   - **Fix:** Steps down the layout tuning vector $\theta = (\text{margin\_y}, \text{linespread}, \text{sec\_vspace}, \text{itemsep})$ from Level 0 (`top=0.28in, linespread=0.93, sec=3.5pt, itemsep=0.6pt`) to Level 1 (`top=0.24in, linespread=0.90, sec=2.5pt, itemsep=0.3pt`) or Level 2 (`top=0.22in, linespread=0.88, sec=2.0pt, itemsep=0.25pt`).
3. **Case C — Structural Line Overflow ($N_{\text{pages}} = 2$ with $> 3$ spillover lines):**
   - **Fix:** Identifies any bullet whose character length falls in the "orphan 3rd-line zone" ($235 < L_{\text{chars}} \le 265$, where 1–3 words spill onto a 3rd line) and invokes a targeted LLM micro-compression pass to tighten that bullet to $195 \le L_{\text{chars}} \le 225$ (2 full lines) while preserving all bolded JD keywords and metrics.
4. **Case D — Under-Filled Page ($N_{\text{pages}} = 1$ with $\Phi_{\text{fill}} < 90\%$):**
   - **Fix:** Steps up the layout tuning vector $\theta$ toward more generous vertical breathing room (`linespread=0.93`, `sec_vspace=3.5pt`) or adds a 3rd project entry from the candidate knowledge graph so the bottom margin never looks sparse.

### Module 3: Closed-Loop Single-Page LaTeX Compiler (`src/latex_compiler.py`)

```python
import os
import re
import shutil
import subprocess
from PIL import Image

LAYOUT_LEVELS = [
    {"margin_y": "0.26in", "margin_x": "0.36in", "linespread": "0.92", "sec_v": "2.8pt", "item_v": "0.4pt"},
    {"margin_y": "0.24in", "margin_x": "0.35in", "linespread": "0.90", "sec_v": "2.4pt", "item_v": "0.3pt"},
    {"margin_y": "0.22in", "margin_x": "0.35in", "linespread": "0.88", "sec_v": "2.0pt", "item_v": "0.25pt"},
    {"margin_y": "0.20in", "margin_x": "0.34in", "linespread": "0.86", "sec_v": "1.6pt", "item_v": "0.2pt"},
]

def build_preamble(level: dict) -> str:
    return rf"""\documentclass[10pt,letterpaper]{{article}}
\usepackage[left={level['margin_x']},right={level['margin_x']},top={level['margin_y']},bottom={level['margin_y']}]{{geometry}}
\usepackage{{amsmath,amssymb}}
\usepackage{{hyperref}}
\usepackage{{xcolor}}

\hypersetup{{colorlinks=false, pdfborder={{0 0 0}}}}
\pagestyle{{empty}}
\setlength{{\parindent}}{{0pt}}
\setlength{{\parskip}}{{0pt}}
\linespread{{{level['linespread']}}}

\newcommand{{\ulhref}}[2]{{\href{{#1}}{{\underline{{#2}}}}}}

\newcommand{{\ressection}}[1]{{%
  \vspace{{{level['sec_v']}}}%
  {{\large\textbf{{#1}}}}\par\vspace{{1.1pt}}%
  \hrule height 0.5pt%
  \vspace{{1.4pt}}%
}}

\newcommand{{\roleheader}}[4]{{%
  \vspace{{1.2pt}}%
  {{\small\noindent\textbf{{#1}} \hfill \textbf{{#2}}\par%
  \noindent\textit{{#3}} \hfill \textit{{#4}}\par}}%
  \vspace{{0.3pt}}%
}}

\newcommand{{\eduheader}}[4]{{%
  \vspace{{1.0pt}}%
  {{\small\noindent\textbf{{#1}} \hfill \textbf{{#2}}\par%
  \noindent\textit{{#3}} \hfill \textit{{#4}}\par}}%
}}

\newcommand{{\projheader}}[2]{{%
  \vspace{{1.2pt}}%
  {{\small\noindent#1 \hfill \textit{{#2}}\par}}%
  \vspace{{0.3pt}}%
}}

\newenvironment{{reslist}}{{%
  \begin{{list}}{{--}}{{%
    \setlength{{\leftmargin}}{{1.1em}}%
    \setlength{{\labelwidth}}{{0.75em}}%
    \setlength{{\labelsep}}{{0.35em}}%
    \setlength{{\itemsep}}{{{level['item_v']}}}%
    \setlength{{\parsep}}{{0pt}}%
    \setlength{{\topsep}}{{0.3pt}}%
    \setlength{{\partopsep}}{{0pt}}%
    \small
  }}%
}}{{%
  \end{{list}}%
  \vspace{{0.2pt}}%
}}

\newcommand{{\headerblock}}{{%
  \begin{{center}}
    {{\Huge\scshape Chandra Rup Daka}}\\[2.5pt]
    {{\footnotesize Houston, TX $\cdot$ \ulhref{{tel:12536323181}}{{+1 (253) 632-3181}} $\cdot$ \ulhref{{mailto:chandrarupdaka@gmail.com}}{{chandrarupdaka@gmail.com}} $\cdot$ \ulhref{{https://www.linkedin.com/in/chandra-rup-daka/}}{{linkedin.com/in/chandra-rup-daka}} $\cdot$ \ulhref{{https://github.com/chandrarup}}{{github.com/chandrarup}} $\cdot$ \ulhref{{https://chandrarup.github.io/Portfolio/}}{{chandrarup.github.io}}}}
  \end{{center}}
  \vspace{{-5pt}}
}}
"""

def measure_vertical_fill(png_path: str) -> float:
    img = Image.open(png_path).convert("RGB")
    w, h = img.size
    px = img.load()
    lowest_ink_y = 0
    for y in range(h - 1, 0, -1):
        if any(min(px[x, y]) < 245 for x in range(int(w * 0.05), int(w * 0.95), 4)):
            lowest_ink_y = y
            break
    return round((lowest_ink_y / h) * 100.0, 1)

def compile_and_verify_single_page(body_latex: str, out_dir: str, stem: str) -> dict:
    os.makedirs(out_dir, exist_ok=True)
    for idx, level in enumerate(LAYOUT_LEVELS):
        full_tex = build_preamble(level) + "\n" + body_latex.strip() + "\n"
        tmp_tex = f"/tmp/{stem}.tex"
        tmp_pdf = f"/tmp/{stem}.pdf"
        tmp_log = f"/tmp/{stem}.log"
        with open(tmp_tex, "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
            f.write(full_tex)

        cmd = ["pdflatex", "-interaction=nonstopmode", "-halt-on-error", f"{stem}.tex"]
        res = subprocess.run(cmd, cwd="/tmp", capture_output=True, text=True)
        if res.returncode != 0:
            raise RuntimeError(f"LaTeX syntax error:\n{res.stdout[-1500:]}")
        subprocess.run(cmd, cwd="/tmp", capture_output=True, text=True)

        info = subprocess.run(["pdfinfo", tmp_pdf], capture_output=True, text=True).stdout
        pages = int([l.split(":")[1].strip() for l in info.splitlines() if l.startswith("Pages:")][0])

        log_text = open(tmp_log, encoding="utf-8", errors="ignore").read()
        overfulls = re.findall(r"Overfull \\hbox \(([0-9.]+)pt too wide\)", log_text)
        max_overfull = max([float(x) for x in overfulls], default=0.0)

        if pages == 1:
            subprocess.run(["pdftoppm", "-png", "-r", "150", tmp_pdf, f"/tmp/{stem}_page"], check=True)
            png_path = f"/tmp/{stem}_page-1.png"
            fill_pct = measure_vertical_fill(png_path)
            shutil.copyfile(tmp_tex, os.path.join(out_dir, f"{stem}.tex"))
            shutil.copyfile(tmp_pdf, os.path.join(out_dir, f"{stem}.pdf"))
            return {
                "status": "success",
                "level_used": idx,
                "pages": pages,
                "vertical_fill_pct": fill_pct,
                "max_overfull_pt": max_overfull,
                "pdf_path": os.path.join(out_dir, f"{stem}.pdf"),
                "tex_path": os.path.join(out_dir, f"{stem}.tex"),
            }
    raise RuntimeError("Exceeded 1 page even at Level 3 compression; trigger LLM bullet shortening pass.")
```

## Conclusions and Operational Deployment Roadmap

Building a custom JD-to-Resume engine around a verified Candidate Knowledge Graph and a closed-loop LaTeX compiler resolves the fundamental tension in high-volume technical job hunting: scaling application velocity without sacrificing human authenticity or typographic precision.

To deploy this architecture inside **Antigravity**:

1. **Initialize the Repository with `career_knowledge.json`:** Seed the `data/` directory with the canonical experience, project, publication, and metric records documented in Section 3 of this white paper.
2. **Wire the Two-Call LLM Pipeline:** Configure Call 1 (`jd_analyzer.py`) to extract the structured `DeconstructedJD` JSON and route the posting to one of the five base tracks, and Call 2 (`bullet_synthesizer.py`) to emit the `TailoredResumeAST` enforcing Tsenta's `WHAT + HOW + WHY + WHERE` qualification structure.
3. **Attach the Closed-Loop Compiler (`latex_compiler.py`):** Run `pdflatex`, `pdfinfo`, `pdftoppm`, and the `Pillow` vertical-fill scanner inside `/tmp` so every generated resume is mathematically guaranteed to compile onto a single US Letter page before export.
4. **Integrate with [SmartApplyAI](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/chandrarup/SmartApplyAI):** Point the existing Flask backend of `SmartApplyAI` to invoke `pipeline.py` whenever a new job posting is scraped in Chrome, enabling 1-click generation, side-by-side diff review, and instant PDF attachment across Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby applications.
